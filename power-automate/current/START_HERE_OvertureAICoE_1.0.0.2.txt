Overture AI CoE workflows 1.0.0.2 - adds the AI CoE Approved Tools list to SharePoint Provisioning

Site:     https://osscontact.sharepoint.com/sites/OvertureAICoE (OSS tenant)
Package:  OvertureAICoE_1_0_0_2.zip (built from the governance builder; SHA-256 in the builder's output)
Replaces: OvertureAICoE_1_0_0_1.zip, your live export of 2026-09-30. Its five flows are unchanged except SharePoint
          Provisioning; the other four are byte-for-byte the live definitions.
Not imported, saved or run.

WHAT CHANGED
  SharePoint Provisioning now also creates "AI CoE Approved Tools": one row per AI tool the AI CoE has reviewed
  (status, what it is approved for, eight yes/no information allowances, conditions, not approved for, how to get
  access, last reviewed, reviewed by). Everyone on the site reads every row; only site owners and the "AI CoE
  Operators" group can add or change rows, so nobody can approve their own tool:
    1. the list's permission inheritance is broken, keeping every existing grant (only if it still inherits);
    2. the "AI CoE Operators" site group gets Full Control on the list (if the group is missing, this step is
       skipped and the run carries on; owners can still edit);
    3. Item-level permissions: Read all items / Create and edit: None (ReadSecurity 1, WriteSecurity 4).
  The front door's own installer (New-FrontDoorPages.ps1 -ListsOnly) creates the same list the same way.
  Existing lists and rows are not touched; Provisioning only adds what is missing.

STEPS
  1. make.powerautomate.com > the OSS environment > Solutions > Import > OvertureAICoE_1_0_0_2.zip (it upgrades
     1.0.0.1; keep the same connections).
  2. Open "Overture AI CoE — SharePoint Provisioning", Save, turn On, Run once, confirm success, turn Off.
  3. Check that Intake Processing, Triage and Routing, and Review and Decision are still On (the package carries
     them Off; your live ones were On). Turn any that went Off back On.
  4. On the site: Site contents > AI CoE Approved Tools > List settings > Permissions for this list: Owners and
     AI CoE Operators show Full Control. Advanced settings shows "Read all items" and "Create and Edit access: None".
  5. Add the tools the AI CoE has reviewed. ToolId is a short fixed key (for example m365-copilot-chat); Title is the
     name people use; OtherNames are the other names they might type, comma-separated. A yes/no left blank means no.
